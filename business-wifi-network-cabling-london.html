Business network installs

One network for your staff, your customers and your equipment.

A business network has a few jobs at once. Staff need it for their day-to-day work, customers may want guest Wi-Fi, and fixed kit like tills, card machines, printers and office equipment often works better on a cable. We plan the Wi-Fi and the cabling together so everything has a clear purpose.

Access points (APs) go where the wireless coverage is needed. Staff and guest Wi-Fi can be kept apart, so your team has its own connection and customers use a different one. Where a device is better on a cable, we run proper, organised cabling back to the network instead of loose leads and quick fixes.

We can label the cables so it’s easy to see what each socket or device is connected to. At handover, we walk whoever looks after the site through what’s been installed, which connections serve the key equipment and how the staff and guest networks are set up.

Working around you

We fit the work around your business.

No two sites are the same, so we start with what your business does, how you use the space and which areas and devices need connecting.

If you’re already open, tell us your opening hours and any times when it’s easier for us to get in. It helps us plan access, cable routes and the order we do things.

To help us quote

  • Type of business, rough size and postcode
  • Where you need Wi-Fi, and roughly how many people or devices
  • Any tills, card machines, printers, office kit or CCTV
  • Your opening hours and when you’d like the work done

Business Wi-Fi and cabling FAQs

Questions people usually ask

Can staff and customers use different Wi-Fi networks?

Yes. Your staff network can be kept separate from guest Wi-Fi, so your team and your customers aren’t on the same network. Mention both when you get in touch.

What can be connected with structured cabling?

Usually tills, card machines, printers and office equipment. The layout depends on where your equipment sits and how you use the space.

Can installation fit around opening times?

Tell us your opening hours and any access restrictions, and we’ll talk through when the work can be done.

What happens at handover?

We’ll show you the access points we’ve fitted, how the staff and guest networks are set up, and which labelled connections serve your fixed equipment.

Useful details to include

Photos or a simple sketch of the site help us see the layout, where the equipment could go and possible cable routes. Let us know about any access restrictions or areas that need to stay in use.
