Planning and installation

Useful coverage starts with the right views, not simply more cameras.

Before installation, it helps to decide what the system should show. That may include people approaching an entrance, activity on a driveway or in a garden, or key areas inside a shop, office, salon or workshop. The planned view matters as much as the mounting position: an entrance may need a different angle from a wider overview of a shop floor or stock area.

CityPlug discusses the areas that matter to you, possible mounting points and cable routes that can be kept tidy where practical. Planning also considers where the NVR will sit, how you expect to review footage and whether remote viewing from a phone is required. If night-time coverage is important, say which areas need to remain useful after dark so that this can form part of the planning conversation.

Once the cameras and recorder are connected, the setup includes NVR configuration, recording checks and remote viewing setup where required. The handover is also the point to confirm how you will use the system. Decisions such as recording retention, the views shown on the recorder and who needs remote access should be discussed for the property rather than assumed in advance.

Questions to consider

  • Which people, routes or areas do you need to see?
  • Do you need close coverage, a wider overview or both?
  • Where could the recorder be located?
  • Who needs to view the system remotely?
  • How should recording retention fit your needs?

Preparing an enquiry

You do not need a finished camera plan.

Include the postcode so coverage can be checked. A few photographs or a short description of entrances, external areas and internal spaces can show the shape of the job before the details are discussed.

Information that helps with a quote

  • Property type and postcode area
  • Areas you want the cameras to cover
  • Approximate number of cameras
  • Any known cable-route or recorder-location considerations
  • Whether phone viewing is needed
  • Your preferred availability

CCTV installation FAQs

Questions to settle before installation.

How do we decide where cameras should go?

Start with what you need to see rather than a camera count. Entrances, routes onto the property, driveways, gardens, tills and stock areas can each require a different view. Mounting positions and practical cable routes can then be considered around those priorities.

What is included in the setup?

The service covers wired camera installation, NVR setup, recording checks and remote viewing setup where required. The final handover confirms the agreed views and how you will access the system.

Can I view the cameras from my phone?

Remote viewing can be set up as part of the installation. Mention who needs access when enquiring so this can be included in the setup and handover.

What should I send for a quote?

Include the property type, postcode area, approximate camera count and the main areas you want covered. Add any priorities for night-time viewing, recording retention, NVR location or phone access. If the layout is difficult to describe, photographs can help communicate the scope.
