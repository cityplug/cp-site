Planning and installation

Good coverage comes from the right views, not more cameras.

Start with what you need to see. That might be people coming to the front door, the driveway or garden, or the till and stock room in a shop, office, salon or workshop. The angle matters as much as where the camera’s mounted: a front door needs a different view from a wide shot of a shop floor.

We’ll talk through the areas that matter to you, where cameras could go and how to keep the cable runs tidy. We’ll also work out where the recorder (NVR) will sit, how you’ll look back at footage and whether you want to watch from your phone. If you need to see certain areas clearly at night, tell us and we’ll plan for it.

Once everything’s connected, we set up the recorder, check it’s recording properly and get phone viewing working if you want it. At handover we’ll go through how to use it, and agree things like how long footage is kept, what shows on the recorder screen and who gets access on their phone.

Worth thinking about

  • Who or what do you need to see, and where?
  • Do you want close-up detail, a wide view, or both?
  • Where could the recorder go?
  • Who needs to watch on their phone?
  • Recording retention: how long do you want to keep footage?

CCTV installation FAQs

Questions people usually ask.

How do we decide where cameras should go?

We start with what you need to see, not how many cameras you want. Front doors, paths onto the property, driveways, gardens, tills and stock areas often each need their own view. Then we work out mounting points and cable routes around that.

What’s included?

Wired cameras, the recorder (NVR), recording checks and phone viewing if you want it. At handover we make sure the views are what we agreed and that you know how to get into the system.

Can I view the cameras from my phone?

Yes, we can set that up as part of the install. Tell us who needs access when you get in touch and we’ll include them.

Can I use my own cameras?

Yes, if you’d like to. Tell us the make and model when you get in touch, and we’ll check they’re compatible and right for the job before we plan around them.

What should I send for a quote?

Your property type, postcode area, roughly how many cameras and the main areas you want covered. Mention anything about night-time viewing, how long you want to keep footage, where the recorder could go or phone access. If the layout’s hard to explain, a few photos help.
