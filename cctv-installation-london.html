What we fit

CCTV coverage for entrances, driveways, gardens, tills and stock areas.

Good CCTV is not just about putting cameras up. The useful part is choosing the right coverage, mounting points, cable routes, recorder setup and mobile viewing so the system is easy to use after the install.

CityPlug focuses on wired CCTV where reliability matters: homes, small shops, offices, salons, workshops and landlords who want a clean installation without messy exposed cabling.

  • Wired camera runs with tidy cable routes where practical
  • NVR setup, recording checks and mobile app handover
  • Front door, driveway, garden, shop floor, till and stock-room coverage
