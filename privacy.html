Privacy notice

Privacy and your personal information.

This notice explains what information CityPlug receives when you ask for a quote or contact us, how it is used and who processes it.

Information you provide

When you request a quote or email CityPlug, you may provide your name, email address, optional phone number, the service you need, postcode area, preferred availability and details about the property or work required.

Only include information needed for the enquiry. Do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents or other sensitive information through the quote form.

How we use your information

CityPlug uses your details to respond to the enquiry, check whether the location is covered, understand the work, discuss or prepare a quote and arrange an appointment where required.

For quote and service requests, this processing is needed to take steps at your request before entering into a contract. For other genuine enquiries and related business records, CityPlug relies on its legitimate interest in responding to and managing those enquiries.

How the quote form works

The quote form uses Formspree. When you submit the form, Formspree processes the details on CityPlug's behalf, filters spam and delivers the enquiry to CityPlug.

The enquiry is delivered to info@cityplug.co.uk, and a copy is sent to a private CityPlug Discord channel used to manage enquiries. Discord therefore processes the same form details.

Read Formspree's privacy policy and Discord's privacy policy for information about how they process submitted data.

Who we share information with

Apart from Formspree, Discord and providers needed to host and secure the website, enquiry information stays within CityPlug. It is not shared with another business unless you ask CityPlug to do so or disclosure is required by law.

How long we keep it

CityPlug keeps enquiry and quotation records for six months after the last contact, then deletes them.

Cookies and technical information

CityPlug does not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website. The hosting provider may process basic technical request information when you visit, and Formspree processes technical information when you submit the quote form.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for a copy of your personal information or ask for it to be corrected, deleted or restricted. You may also object to certain uses of your information.

To make a privacy request, email info@cityplug.co.uk. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Contact

CityPlug decides how personal information submitted through this website is used. For privacy questions or requests, email info@cityplug.co.uk.

This notice was last updated on 29 August 2026. It will be reviewed if the website or enquiry process changes.
