Privacy notice

How CityPlug uses information sent through this website.

This notice explains what we collect when you contact CityPlug, why we need it and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or email us, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, postcode area, availability and the details you provide about the property or work required.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive information through the quote form.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to understand the work requested, check whether we cover the location, prepare or discuss a quote, arrange an appointment and respond to related questions. We process this information to take steps at your request before providing a service and for our legitimate interest in managing genuine business enquiries.

Quote-form provider

The website quote form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. Formspree receives the submitted information so it can deliver the enquiry to CityPlug and provide spam protection. You can read Formspree's privacy policy for information about its own processing.

Enquiry delivery — confirm before publication

CityPlug's form setup documentation includes delivery to info@cityplug.co.uk and an optional private Discord channel used to manage enquiries. CityPlug must confirm whether Discord forwarding is active before this notice is published. If it is active, the submitted name, contact details, postcode area, availability and job details are forwarded to Discord and are intended to be visible only to authorised members of that private channel. Read Discord's privacy policy for information about its own processing.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell enquiry information. We share it only with service providers needed to receive, secure or respond to the enquiry, or where disclosure is required by law. We keep enquiry and quotation records only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain appropriate business records and resolve disputes.

Cookies and analytics

CityPlug does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies on this website. Basic technical request information may still be processed by the hosting provider and by Formspree when you submit the form.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access to your personal information or request that it is corrected, deleted or restricted. You may also object to certain processing. To make a privacy request, email info@cityplug.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Contact and updates

CityPlug is responsible for information submitted through this website. Privacy enquiries can be sent to info@cityplug.co.uk. This notice was last updated on 27 August 2026 and will be revised if the website's data handling changes.
